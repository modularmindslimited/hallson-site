Who we are

The building arm of a family developer.

Hallson Construction LLP is the sister company of Hallson Homes, a family developer of new homes in the South East. Hallson Homes designs each site to suit its location; Hallson Construction builds it.

Keeping construction in the family means the same people are accountable from the first drawing to the finished home.
